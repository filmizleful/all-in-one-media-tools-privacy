Privacy Policy - All-in-One Media Tools

Last updated: October 23, 2026

All-in-One Media Tools is developed by Guney Apps. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the application.

1. Local Media and File Processing

The application provides tools for working with photos, videos, audio files, documents and other files stored on your device.

Photos, videos, audio files and documents selected or managed by the user are processed locally on the device. Guney Apps does not operate a server that uploads or stores these files.

Some features may request access to device storage or media when required for functions such as viewing, organizing, copying, moving, renaming, converting, editing or exporting files.

2. Advertising

The application uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect or process information including:

  • Approximate location derived from information such as IP address
  • Device or advertising identifiers
  • Application interactions
  • Diagnostic information

This information may be used by Google for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security and compliance purposes.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Accounts

All-in-One Media Tools does not require users to create an account and does not provide a user account registration system.

4. Data Transmission

Data transmitted by third-party services such as Google Mobile Ads is transmitted using secure network connections.

5. Third-Party Services

The application may use Google services and software development kits required for advertising and application functionality. These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

All-in-One Media Tools is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Guney Apps does not maintain user accounts or a developer-operated server containing users' personal media files.

Files created, edited or managed by the application remain on the user's device unless the user chooses to delete or share them.

Information processed by third-party services such as Google is subject to the retention and deletion practices of those services.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features, services or legal requirements change. The latest version will always be available on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions regarding All-in-One Media Tools, users may contact Guney Apps through the developer contact information provided on the application's Google Play Store listing.
